Grid Desk

Learn the network by doing the job.

Each desk is one job at a network business: assessing a connection, watching frequency, ranking assets for replacement. You learn what the job needs from AEMO's NEM Foundations course and TasNetworks' Annual Planning Report 2026, use a tool modelled on the software engineers use, and finish with a note you write yourself.

  1. Briefthe job, as a work ticket
  2. Learnthe ideas, with course and report pages
  3. Dothe tool, on published figures
  4. Produceyour note, in your own words
  5. Checkquestions answered with Claude

The rules that keep this honest. Published figures carry their source and page; simulations are labelled [SIMULATED]. No desk pretends to be TasNetworks' real network or its real assessments: their network model isn't public. Every desk says what it shows and what it doesn't. Your notes are yours: drafts stay in your browser until you choose to publish them. Measurements happen in the lab.
