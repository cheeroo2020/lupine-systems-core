Our approach

Principles of evidence

Five rules that shape everything Lupine builds.

They come from one hard question: when software asks equipment to do something and the line goes quiet, what can it honestly say happened?

  1. 01Missing is never zero.

    A sensor that sends nothing has not reported 0 kW. Treating silence as zero can "confirm" a shutdown that never happened. Textbook 01-18 · src/rail_lite/outcome.py

  2. 02An acknowledgement is not an outcome.

    "Accepted" means the message arrived. Only a fresh reading of the right quantity shows the equipment did it. Textbook 06-16

  3. 03Uncertain stays uncertain until new evidence.

    A timeout is a fact about waiting, not about the equipment. Only reconciling with fresh evidence can resolve it. Textbook 06-17, 06-19

  4. 04Never resend while the first may have run.

    If the reply was lost, the action may already have happened. Sending again can do it twice. Textbook 06-18, 06-20 · safe_to_reissue()

  5. 05Integrity is not truth.

    A tamper-evident record proves the record wasn't changed. It can't prove the reading was accurate, or that a command caused an effect. Textbook 06-25 · src/cloked_lite/
