How Tasmania's electricity system is run, learned by building it.

I'm Chris Gogoi. This site follows how AEMO runs the National Electricity Market, how TasNetworks plans and operates Tasmania's transmission and distribution networks, and where their responsibilities meet. Each piece is built by me, checked against AEMO's NEM Foundations course and TasNetworks' Annual Planning Report 2026, and added only when it's finished.
