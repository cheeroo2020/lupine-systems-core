§ 01 · THE PROBLEM

Most cross-border money
takes the wrong route.

Every day, trillions move across borders — and nearly every transfer is routed on intuition, not evidence. Payroll picks the cheapest rail even when speed matters. Treasury picks the fastest even when it's overkill.

Lupine replaces that guesswork with a single, transparent decision. It reads your urgency, scores every viable path on speed, cost and reliability, and signs the outcome with a cryptographic receipt.

WHAT CHANGES

§ 02 · THE ENGINE

Three layers.
One decision.

01 🧠
AIVA · INTELLIGENCE

Thinks about your move.

Weighs speed, cost and reliability against urgency. Scores every route using a composite utility function — and picks the winner.

02 ⚡
RAIL · EXECUTION

Chooses the path.

Simulates SWIFT, NPP, RTGS and local clearing. Selects the rail that matches your timeline and budget — not the default one.

03 🔐
CLOKED · EVIDENCE

Signs the decision.

SHA-256 hash chain locks every move. An unbreakable audit trail — proof of why your money went the way it did.

COMPOSITE UTILITY SCORE
U = w1·Speed + w2·Cost + w3·Reliability

Weights shift with urgency. Critical transfers lean on speed; routine ones lean on cost.
