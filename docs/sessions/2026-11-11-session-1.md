**The question:** How does electricity get from a generator through the network to a customer in Tasmania,
and who is responsible at each point?
**Time:** about 75 minutes. Pen and paper are enough; no computer work today.

## 1. Predict (5 minutes, before reading anything)

On a blank page, draw **five boxes in a row**, from a hydro power station to a house in Hobart. Under each box, write:

- **who you think owns or runs it.**

Don't look anything up. Wrong guesses are useful: they show what the reading changes.
