# Plan: job-ready by Saturday 21 November 2026

**"Job-ready" here means:** ready to apply and interview for entry-level network roles (network planning support,
asset performance, engineering support, connections support). It doesn't mean ready to work as an electrical engineer.

**By 21 November you'll have:**
- **The three foundations, explained unaided:**
  1. from generator to customer, and who is responsible at each point;
  2. how AEMO balances and dispatches, and how a network limit changes the outcome;
  3. how TasNetworks plans capacity and screens new loads.
- **The Connection Lens:** your traceable screen of a load near Hobart, built with you, plus a one-page case study.
- **A CV** that names finished work only.
- **Practised answers** to the questions you're likely to be asked.
- **One or two applications** ready to send.

**Time:** about 15 hours over the ten days, plus your slides.

**The rule that still holds:** move on when you can explain the current step, not when the calendar says so. If a
step needs more time, it takes it. On 21 November you apply with whatever is genuinely finished.
